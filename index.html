Frequently Asked Questions

What is your typical project timeline?

Most projects are completed within 4-6 weeks, depending on complexity. We follow a structured process: 1 week for design approval, 2-3 weeks for development, and 1-2 weeks for testing and refinement.

What's included in your maintenance packages?

Our maintenance packages include regular updates, security monitoring, performance optimization, content updates, and technical support. We offer 24/7 emergency support for critical issues.

Do you provide SEO services?

Yes, all our websites are built with SEO best practices. We also offer advanced SEO packages including keyword research, content optimization, and monthly performance reporting.

Can you help with content creation?

Absolutely! We have in-house copywriters and content strategists who can help create compelling content that engages your audience and drives conversions.

Our projects typically range from €5,000 to €25,000 depending on requirements. We provide detailed quotes after understanding your specific needs and objectives.
